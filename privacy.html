TOKYO BUCHIAGE Uploader

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 22, 2026

Personal tool. This tool is used by one creator to prepare uploads to that creator’s own TikTok account. It does not provide a public service or sell personal data.

Information handled

The tool stores TikTok authorization tokens locally on the owner’s computer. It reads locally selected video files and sends them to TikTok only after the owner confirms an upload.

How information is used

Authorization tokens are used solely to request TikTok uploads for the account that granted permission. Video files are used solely to create TikTok drafts. The tool does not collect analytics, contacts, location data, or payment information.

Sharing

Information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Video files and account authorization are sent to TikTok only when the owner explicitly initiates an upload, and are then subject to TikTok’s own policies.

Security and retention

Tokens remain on the owner’s local computer and can be revoked through TikTok or removed by deleting the local tool data. The owner is responsible for keeping the computer and its files secure.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the creator through the associated TOKYO BUCHIAGE SYNDROME channel.
