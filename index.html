BY TOKYO BUCHI♥AGE SYNDROME

A focused TikTok upload workflow for original music videos.

TOKYO BUCHIAGE Uploader is a personal desktop tool used by its creator to prepare original video files and upload them to the creator’s authorized TikTok account.

It is not a public publishing service. Every upload and final publishing decision remains under the creator’s control.

ABOUT

What the tool does

The tool supports a clear, creator-led workflow for posting original music video content. It connects only to the TikTok account that the creator authorizes, works with video files selected on the creator’s computer, and sends a video to TikTok when the creator initiates an upload.

It does not make editorial decisions, publish without confirmation, or represent a platform for other users.

FEATURES

Workflow capabilities

Account authorization

Connect the creator’s own TikTok account through TikTok authorization.

Video selection

Select original video files stored locally on the creator’s computer.

Upload preparation

Review the video and the relevant upload details before sending it to TikTok.

Creator-controlled posting

The creator reviews the result in TikTok and retains responsibility for every final posting choice.

HOW IT WORKS

From source video to TikTok

  1. AuthorizeThe creator authorizes their TikTok account.
  2. SelectThe creator chooses an original, locally stored video.
  3. ReviewThe creator reviews the upload information and starts the upload.
  4. UploadThe video is sent to TikTok through the authorized workflow.
  5. Publish responsiblyThe creator reviews TikTok settings and completes any final publishing action in TikTok.
